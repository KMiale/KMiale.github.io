OptiTap® Compatible Flat Drop Cable for FTTH and Broadband Networks

LYNN OptiTap Compatible Flat Drop Cables provide a straightforward option for outside plant teams that need consistent mechanical performance in tight spaces. The singlemode OS2 design is available in toneable and non toneable versions and supports standard OptiTap style architectures.


Key Characteristics

The ruggedized 3 mm core resists kinks and breakage during pulls and routing through ducts and enclosures.

The outer jacket removes cleanly while the 3 mm cable remains intact for predictable connectorization.

Assemblies are built to order in the United States with short production lead times.

An H style connector interface mates with OptiTap products from major vendors.


Performance Specifications
Fiber Type            Singlemode Flat Drop, OS2
Insertion Loss        Typical 0.3 dB
Macro Bending Loss    Less than 0.75 dB at 1550 nm, 10 mm radius
Minimum Bend Radius   30 mm long term, 60 mm short term
Tensile Resistance    400 N long term, 1350 N short term
Operating Temperature -40 °C to +70 °C
LYNN OptiTap Flat Drop Diagram


Construction Overview

1.9 mm tight buffer fiber (G657A1) with aramid yarn inside an LSZH inner sheath.

FRP glass fiber strength members and optional bare copper toning conductor.

Polyethylene outer sheath supporting outdoor environments.


Standards and Approvals

ITU-T G.652.D, G.657A1 · EN 60811-1-1 · EN 60974-1-2-E1 · EN 60974-1-2-E3
