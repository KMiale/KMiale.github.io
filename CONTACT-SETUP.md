# Contact form setup

The form posts to FormSubmit for delivery to katimiale@gmail.com. Required fields: business email and first name. Optional fields: last name and phone. Email format is validated by the browser; personal email domains are accepted so independent business owners can inquire. reCAPTCHA stays enabled and a honeypot is included.

After publishing at https://kmiale.github.io/, submit the form once using your own details. Open the FormSubmit activation email in Gmail and click the confirmation link. Check spam if necessary. Then submit a second test and confirm the inquiry arrives. Delivery has not been tested from this workspace and requires that activation.

Upload thank-you.html alongside index.html. If the website domain changes, update the _next and _url values in index.html.
