# Kati Miale — Brand reference
Established November 12, 2026. Use this reference for future website and marketing work.

## Positioning
Kati Miale — Fractional CMO for B2B Technology.
Brand promise: “Build the marketing function your next stage of growth requires.”
Primary line: “Built to launch. Accountable for growth.”
Supporting line: “Senior leadership. AI-enabled execution.”
Campaign headline: “Build for the next stage.”
Perspective: “The goal is growth. AI expands what your team can deliver.”

## Audience and character
B2B technology companies preparing to launch or grow.
Direct, commercially minded, technically fluent and resourceful.
Connect AI-enabled execution to capacity and business outcomes. Include startups that have yet to generate revenue.

## Visual direction
Ink navy, warm white and restrained Pacific blue.
Strong sans serif typography, generous spacing and an editorial feel.
KM monogram with adequate space around it.
Use concrete examples of marketing work and outcomes.

Website implementation tokens (selected to express the earlier palette; original artwork did not specify exact hex codes):

The website KM is a typographic interpretation; the original logo artwork has not been embedded.

## Writing
Concise, clear, specific, confident and focused on results.
No series of three. No negative or contrast statements. Never use “actually.”
Avoid dashes and ellipses in marketing copy.
Do not invent metrics or present company revenue growth as solely attributable to marketing.
